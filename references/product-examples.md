# Contextual product examples

These three profiles are specification fixtures that show why design judgment must follow a product's job and approved Design DNA. They are not live inspections, verified current specifications, or permission to edit the named repositories. For another product, infer its own identity from approved evidence rather than copying one of these directions.

## Farvo

- **Example direction:** Playful, colorful, soft, rounded, warm, and suited to routine shopping.
- **Preserve when verified:** Approved mascots and icon identity, product variants, quantities, prices, delivery facts, and the product's cheerful voice.
- **Possible design emphasis:** Friendly controls, clear product choices, quick mobile comparison, and a visible order summary.
- **Avoid:** Turning the product into monochrome financial software, hiding cut/weight/price details, or treating all color, borders, and character as clutter.

## Habis

- **Example direction:** Restrained, minimal, professional, calm, and trustworthy.
- **Preserve when verified:** Financial terms, authorized scope, fees, status truth, recovery, consent, dispute, and user-visible commitments.
- **Possible design emphasis:** Precise hierarchy, clear amounts, transparent transaction state, quiet visual treatment, and legible recovery paths.
- **Avoid:** Hiding obligations to make a screen look empty, suggesting authorization means settlement, or changing financial behavior as an incidental design edit.

## Swapme

- **Example direction:** Expressive, social, emotional, dramatic, story-led, and photo-led.
- **Preserve when verified:** Canonical story facts, correct character and image identity, recognizable social actions, and unresolved outcomes until the system resolves them.
- **Possible design emphasis:** Story context first, lively but coherent participation controls, and discussion that retains orientation.
- **Avoid:** Replacing story with a generic dashboard, fabricating events or outcomes, or flattening approved expression into sterile cards.

## Use the contrast

The same request, such as “make this cleaner,” should result in different decisions:

- Farvo may reduce competing promotion while keeping warmth and product-choice facts.
- Habis may remove ornament while keeping exact terms and truthful state.
- Swapme may reduce repetitive controls around a story while retaining its emotional and visual character.

If the same palette, card structure, icon family, density, and motion are prescribed to all three without evidence, re-check whether a generic template has replaced contextual judgment.
