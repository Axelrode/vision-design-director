# Anti-AI-Slop Intelligence

Use this discipline to identify unnecessary complexity, generic styling, redundant language, decorative patterns, or poorly judged hierarchy. “Slop” is an unearned decision, not a visual material or style.

Ask whether each element helps the user's task, communicates needed meaning or trust, supports accessibility, expresses approved personality, or satisfies policy. Check dependencies and evidence before changing it. Unknown purpose is a reason to inspect, not delete.

## Diagnose with context

Look for card nesting without grouping value, labels repeated without a role, filler or superlative copy, competing primary actions, arbitrary colors, inconsistent typography, meaningless icons, excessive or obstructive motion, generic dashboard layouts in a different kind of product, hidden interaction states, and repetitive confirmation steps.

Any of these can be useful when it serves a real task or identity. A divider can aid dense financial scanning. A character can carry brand. A badge can show verified status. A photograph can carry the story. Test the purpose instead of applying a blanket deletion rule.

Before removing or simplifying, check task value, meaning, trust, accessibility, personality, evidence, dependencies, and scope. Pay extra attention to fees, consent, labels, errors, recovery, status truth, legal terms, canonical story facts, and approved brand assets. Do not invent urgency, scarcity, completion, or success.

## Choose the smallest defensible action

Use **KEEP**, **SIMPLIFY**, **MERGE**, **MOVE**, **REMOVE**, **REPLACE**, **ESCALATE**, or **NO_CHANGE**. Explain why the element earns or does not earn its place, what must remain, and what evidence supports the recommendation. A heuristic suspicion is not an observed defect. An audit request never authorizes edits.

Success is clearer hierarchy, lower needless effort, and preserved product identity—not fewer colors, cards, words, or files. Stop when the meaningful issue is solved; do not polish indefinitely to satisfy an abstract preference.
